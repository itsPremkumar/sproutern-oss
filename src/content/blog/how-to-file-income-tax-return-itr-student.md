# How to File ITR (Income Tax Return) for Students & Freelancers

"I don't earn enough to pay tax, so why should I file a return?"

This is a common myth. Filing an ITR is not just about paying tax; it's about **claiming refunds**
and **building financial history**. If you are a student doing paid internships or a freelancer
earning gig money, this guide is for you.

---

## Why File ITR (Even with Zero Tax)?

1.  **Get Refund (TDS):** Companies often deduct 10% TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) when paying
    freelancers/interns. If your total income is below taxable limit (₹7 Lakhs), you can get **100%
    of that money back** only by filing ITR.
2.  **Visa Processing:** Countries like US/UK/Canada ask for 3 years' ITR as proof of ties to home
    country.
3.  **Loans:** Banks require ITR as income proof for car/home loans.
4.  **Carry Forward Losses:** Made a loss in stocks? File ITR to carry it forward and adjust against
    future profits.

---

## Which ITR Form to Choose?

- **ITR-1 (Sahaj):** For Salary income + One House Property + Other sources (Interest). _Use if you
  are just an intern with a stipend._
- **ITR-2:** If you have Capital Gains (Stocks/Mutual Funds sales) or Foreign Assets.
- **ITR-3:** For Business income / Freelancers (Regular method).
- **ITR-4 (Sugam):** **The Best for Freelancers.** Presumptive Taxation Scheme (Section 44ADA).

---

## For Freelancers: The Magic of Section 44ADA

If you are a freelancer (Code, Design, Writer, Tutor), the government assumes your "profit" is only
50% of your revenue.

- **Scenario:** You earned ₹10 Lakhs.
- **Benefit:** You declare profit as ₹5 Lakhs (50%).
- **Tax:** You pay tax only on ₹5 Lakhs. Since income up to ₹7 Lakhs is rebate-free (Old Regime ₹5L
  / New Regime ₹7L), you pay **ZERO Tax**.

**Eligibility:** Professionals (Engineers, Doctors, Architects, Interiors, Technical Consultants).

---

## Step-by-Step Filing Process (Self-File)

You don't always need a CA. The new Income Tax Portal is user-friendly.

1.  **Login:** Go to `incometax.gov.in`. Login with PAN.
2.  **AIS/TIS:** Check "Annual Information Statement." This shows what the govt _already knows_ you
    earned (TDS, Bank Interest, Stock sales).
3.  **Select Form:** Choose ITR-1 or ITR-4 based on profile.
4.  **Fill Details:**
    - _Gross Total Income:_ Enter salary/freelance income.
    - _Deductions (80C):_ Enter LIC/PPF investments (Old Regime).
    - _TDS:_ Verify the TDS deducted matches your Form 26AS.
5.  **Verify:** Using Aadhaar OTP.
6.  **Wait:** Refund usually credits in 20-45 days.

---

## Old Regime vs. New Regime (FY 2025-26)

- **New Regime (Default):** No deductions allowed (80C, HRA gone). Lower tax rates. Tax-free up to
  **₹7 Lakhs** (Standard Deduction included for salaried). Simple.
- **Old Regime:** High tax rates but allows deductions (80C, HRA, Medical). Tax-free up to **₹5
  Lakhs**.
- **Verdict:** For most students/freelancers with income < ₹7 Lakhs, **New Regime** is better and
  simpler.

---

## Important Dates

- **Financial Year (FY):** April 1 to March 31. (When you earn).
- **Assessment Year (AY):** The year _after_ FY. (When you file).
- **Deadline:** Usually **July 31st**.
- **Late Fee:** ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 if missed.

---

## Key Takeaways

1.  **Check 26AS:** Before filing, always check Form 26AS/AIS to see how much Tax has already been
    cut from your payments.
2.  **Declare EVERYTHING:** Savings account interest, stock dividends, stipend. AI tracks everything
    now.
3.  **ITR-4 is Gold:** Freelancers save massive tax using Section 44ADA.
4.  **File Nil Return:** Even if income is 0, having the document helps in future financial life.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Stipend taxable?

Yes, it is treated as "Income from Other Sources" or "Salary" depending on the contract. If total
income > ₹3L (New Regime basic exemption), you must file.

### Dealing with Crypto tax?

Flat 30% tax on profit. No slabs. No deductions. 1% TDS on every trade. This is separate from your
slab tax.

### Can I file myself?

Yes, for simple cases (Salary/Freelance). If you have complex capital gains or foreign income, hire
a CA or use apps like ClearTax.
