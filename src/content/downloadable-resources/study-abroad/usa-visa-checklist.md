# USA F1 Student Visa Checklist

> **Important:** Organize your documents in a folder with clear dividers.

## 1. Mandatory Documents

- [ ] **Passport** (Original, valid for at least 6 months beyond stay)
- [ ] **Form I-20** (Signed by you and university official)
- [ ] **DS-160 Confirmation Page** (with barcode)
- [ ] **SEVIS Fee Receipt** (I-901 Receipt)
- [ ] **Visa Interview Appointment Confirmation**
- [ ] **Passport-size Photographs** (2x2 inches, white background, if required)

## 2. Academic Documents

- [ ] **Admission Letter** from the University
- [ ] **GRE/GMAT Score Reports**
- [ ] **TOEFL/IELTS/PTE Score Reports**
- [ ] **Degree Certificates** (Bachelor's and others)
- [ ] **Official Transcripts** (Mark sheets for all semesters)
- [ ] **Resume/CV**

## 3. Financial Documents (Proof of Funds)

- [ ] **Bank Account Statements** (Last 6 months, Savings)
- [ ] **Affidavit of Support** (from parents/sponsors)
- [ ] **Loan Sanction Letter** (if applicable)
- [ ] **Wait... Income Tax Returns (ITR)** (Last 3 years for sponsor)
- [ ] **Salary Slips** (Last 3 months for sponsor)
- [ ] **CA Statement/Asset Valuation Report** (Optional, but helpful)

## 4. Work Experience (If applicable)

- [ ] **Offer Letter/Joining Letter** from previous employers
- [ ] **Relieving Letter/Experience Certificate**
- [ ] **Pay Slips** (Last 3 months)

## 5. Other

- [ ] **SOP (Statement of Purpose)**
- [ ] **List of other universities applied to**
