# UK Student (Tier 4) Visa Checklist

> **Note:** Ensure your financial documents meet the strict "28-day rule".

## 1. Essentials

- [ ] **Passport** (Current and previous)
- [ ] **CAS Statement** (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) - Printout
- [ ] **Visa Application Form** (Printout)
- [ ] **IHS Surcharge Reference Number**
- [ ] **Biometric Appointment Confirmation**

## 2. Financial Evidence

- [ ] **Bank Statements**
  - _Must show full tuition + living expenses held for 28 consecutive days._
  - _Statement date must be within 31 days of application._
- [ ] **Education Loan Letter** (if applicable)
  - _Must be regulated by national banking regulator._
- [ ] **Consent Letter from Parents** (if using their funds)
- [ ] **Birth Certificate** (to prove relationship with parents)

## 3. Health & Travel

- [ ] **TB (Tuberculosis) Test Certificate** (from approved clinic)
- [ ] **ATAS Certificate** (if course requires it)

## 4. Academics

- [ ] **Degree Certificates / Transcripts** (listed on CAS)
- [ ] **English Language Proficiency** (IELTS/PTE or University assessment)

## 5. Credibility Interview Prep (If asked)

- [ ] Know your course modules in detail
- [ ] Explanation of study gap (if any)
- [ ] Reason for choosing UK over other countries
