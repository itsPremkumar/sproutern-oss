# Interview Preparation Worksheet

## The STAR Method

Use this structure for behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time...").

- **S - Situation:** Set the scene and give the necessary details of your example.
- **T - Task:** Describe what your responsibility was in that situation.
- **A - Action:** Explain exactly what steps you took to address it.
- **R - Result:** Share what outcomes your actions achieved.

## 20 Common Interview Questions

1.  Tell me about yourself.
2.  Why do you want to work for us?
3.  What are your greatest strengths?
4.  What is your biggest weakness?
5.  Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
6.  Tell me about a time you showed leadership.
7.  Describe a difficult work situation and how you overcame it.
8.  Why should we hire you?
9.  Do you have any questions for us?
10. What is your salary expectation?
11. How do you handle stress/pressure?
12. Describe a time you made a mistake.
13. What motivates you?
14. How do you prioritize work?
15. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a boss/colleague.
16. What are you passionate about?
17. Why are you leaving your current job?
18. What is your dream job?
19. Describe your work style.
20. Tell me about an accomplishment you are most proud of.
