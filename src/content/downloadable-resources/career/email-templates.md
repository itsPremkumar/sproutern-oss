# Professional Email Templates Pack

## 1. Cold Email for Internship

**Subject:** Internship Inquiry - [Your Role] - [Your Name]

Dear [Hiring Manager Name],

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is [Your Name], and I am a final-year [Major] student at [University Name]. I have been
following [Company Name]'s work on [Specific Project/Product] and was particularly inspired by
[Specific Detail].

I have developed strong skills in [Skill 1], [Skill 2], and [Skill 3]. Recently, I built [Project
Name] which solved [Problem], resulting in [Result]. You can view my portfolio here: [Link].

I am writing to express my interest in a [Role Name] internship opportunity at your company. I
believe my background in [Topic] would allow me to contribute effectively to your [Specific Team].

I have attached my resume for your review. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,

[Your Name] [LinkedIn Profile] [Phone Number]

---

## 2. Asking for a Referral (Alumni)

**Subject:** Student from [University Name] - Interest in [Company]

Hi [Alumni Name],

I hope you’re having a great week.

I’m [Your Name], a junior [Major] student at [University Name]. I noticed you are currently working
as a [Role] at [Company Name]. I saw your profile on LinkedIn and was impressed by your transition
from [College] to working on [Company's Product].

I am very interested in the [Open Role] at [Company Name] and would love to ask a few questions
about your experience there and the company culture. Would you be open to a brief 10-minute chat or
email exchange?

I truly appreciate your time and advice.

Best regards,

[Your Name]

---

## 3. Post-Interview Thank You Note

**Subject:** Thank you - [Your Name] - [Role Name] Interview

Dear [Interviewer Name],

Thank you so much for taking the time to interview me earlier today for the [Role Name] position.

I really enjoyed learning more about [Company Name]’s plans to [Specific Goal mentioned in
interview] and discussing how my experience with [Your Skill] aligns with the team's needs. Our
conversation reinforced my excitement about the opportunity to join your team.

Please let me know if there is any additional information I can provide. I look forward to hearing
from you regarding the next steps.

Best regards,

[Your Name]

---

## 4. Following Up After No Response

**Subject:** Re: Interview for [Role Name] - [Your Name]

Dear [Recruiter Name],

I hope you’re having a good week.

I’m writing to follow up on my interview for the [Role Name] position on [Date]. I remain very
interested in the opportunity and would love to know if there are any updates regarding the hiring
status.

Please let me know if you need any further details from my end.

Best regards,

[Your Name]

---

## 5. Requesting an Informational Interview

**Subject:** Aspiring [Role] - seeking your advice

Hi [Name],

My name is [Your Name] and I am an aspiring [Role]. I’ve been following your work on
[Platform/Project] and really admire your approach to [Specific Topic].

I am currently building my skills in [Skill] and would value your perspective on how the industry is
evolving. If you have a moment, I would appreciate the chance to briefly hear about your journey and
any advice you might have for someone starting out.

I understand you are busy, so even a short email reply would mean a lot.

Thanks,

[Your Name] [Portfolio Link]
