# ATS-Friendly Resume Template & Guide

## What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by recruiters to filter resumes before a human
reads them. It parses your resume for keywords, skills, and formatting. If your resume isn't
ATS-friendly, you might get rejected automatically.

## Rules for ATS Resumes

1.  **Format:** Use a single-column layout. Avoid tables, graphics, and text boxes.
2.  **Font:** Use standard web-safe fonts (Arial, Calibri, Roboto, Open Sans).
3.  **Headings:** Use standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills) – do not get "creative"
    here.
4.  **Keywords:** Mirror the language of the job description.
5.  **File Type:** DOCX or PDF (generated from a text document, not an image).

## Content Checklist

- [ ] Contact info is up to date.
- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors (use Grammarly).
- [ ] Verbs are in the past tense for past roles.
- [ ] Dates are consistent (e.g., "Jan 2023" or "01/2023").
- [ ] PDF text is selectable (not an image).
