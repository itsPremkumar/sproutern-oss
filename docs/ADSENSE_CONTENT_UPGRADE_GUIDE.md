## 🎯 WHAT ADSENSE REVIEWERS LOOK FOR

### Primary Factors:

1. **Sufficient Content** - 30+ pages with substantial content
2. **Original Content** - Not copied from other sites
3. **User Value** - Content that genuinely helps users
4. **Professional Design** - Clean, trustworthy appearance
5. **Clear Purpose** - Obvious what the site does
6. **Legal Compliance** - Privacy policy, terms, contact info
7. **Good UX** - Easy navigation, fast loading
8. **Regular Updates** - Fresh content, updated dates

### Red Flags to Avoid:

- ❌ Thin content (pages with <300 words)
- ❌ Duplicate content
- ❌ Copied content from other sites
- ❌ Too many ads (if reapplying)
- ❌ Misleading content
- ❌ Adult/illegal content
- ❌ Broken links
- ❌ Poor mobile experience
- ❌ Slow loading times
- ❌ No contact information

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR APPROVAL

1. **Quality Over Quantity**: Better to have 30 excellent pages than 100 thin pages

2. **India-Specific is Your Strength**: Emphasize Indian context, cities, colleges, companies

3. **Show Expertise**: Author bios, case studies, detailed guides prove you know your stuff

4. **Update Regularly**: Add "Last Updated" dates and actually update content monthly

5. **Internal Linking**: Connect related content to show depth and keep users engaged

6. **Real Examples**: Use real names, real companies, real numbers (with permission)

7. **Mobile-First**: Most Indian users are on mobile - ensure perfect mobile experience

8. **Fast Loading**: Indian internet can be slow - optimize for speed

9. **Local Language**: Consider adding Hindi translations for key pages (future)

10. **Community Building**: Show active community through testimonials, comments, engagement
