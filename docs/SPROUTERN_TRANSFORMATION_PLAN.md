## SECTION 1 — FULL WEBSITE ANALYSIS (UI/UX + SEO + CONTENT)

After analyzing your file structure and existing content, here is a summary of findings:

- **Homepage Issues:** The current homepage components (`hero`, `features`, `faq`) are a good start
  but lack dynamic content. The page is static and doesn't immediately showcase the site's value.
  - **Improvement:** Add sections for "Latest Internships," "Trending Blog Articles," and "Popular
    Tools" directly on the homepage to increase engagement and demonstrate the site's activity.

- **Navigation Problems:** While essential pages exist, the navigation isn't optimized for user
  journey or SEO. Key, high-value pages are likely buried.
  - **Improvement:** Implement the hierarchical navigation proposed in Section 3 to create clear
    pathways for users and search engine crawlers.

- **Missing High-SEO Pages:** The site is missing high-intent, high-volume page categories that
  competitors leverage.
  - **Improvement:** Create dedicated, indexable pages for **Company-wise Reviews & Interview
    Questions** (e.g., `/companies/tcs/interview-questions`), **Comparison Pages** (e.g.,
    `Wipro vs. Infosys Fresher Salary`), and **Off-Campus Drive Listings**.

- **Missing Trust Pages:** Your project includes the necessary pages like `About`, `Contact`,
  `Privacy Policy`, and `Terms & Conditions`. This is excellent.
  - **Improvement:** Ensure these pages are comprehensive. The `About Us` page should tell a story,
    and the `Contact Us` page should include a physical address (even a virtual one) and business
    email to boost trust signals for AdSense.

- **Missing Blog Categories:** Your blog posts are strong but lack a clear categorical structure,
  making it hard for users to find related content.
  - **Improvement:** Organize all current and future articles into the categories outlined in
    Section 4 (e.g., `Interview Prep`, `High CPC`, `Student Lifestyle`).

- **Engagement Issues:** The primary engagement blocker is the lack of interactive elements. Session
  time and return visits are likely low because users have nothing to _do_ on the site.
  - **Improvement:** Introduce the interactive tools listed in Section 5 (e.g., Resume Score
    Checker, CGPA Converter) to make users stay longer and return often.

- **Technical SEO Problems:** You have `sitemap.ts` and `robots.ts`, which is great. The URL
  structure is clean.
  - **Improvement:** Implement **Schema Markup** across the site. Use `FAQPage` schema for FAQs,
    `BlogPosting` for articles, and create a custom `JobPosting` schema for internships to win rich
    snippets in Google Search. Ensure internal linking is strategic, not just incidental.

- **Missing Monetizable Content (High CPC):** The content is geared towards informational queries,
  not commercial ones.
  - **Improvement:** Create content around high-cost-per-click (CPC) keywords as detailed in Section
    4, such as "Best Data Science Certification in India," "MBA vs. M.Tech," and "Study Abroad
    Scholarships for Indian Students."

- **Missing Student Tools:** This is the biggest gap compared to competitors. Tools are traffic and
  engagement magnets.
  - **Improvement:** Develop and prominently feature the 10 tools from Section 5.

- **Growth Blockers:** The key growth blocker is a lack of a "user retention" loop. Users visit,
  read, and leave.
  - **Improvement:** Implement the engagement features from Section 10 (user logins, dashboards,
    saved items) and the lead magnets from Section 6 to build a loyal user base.

- **Missing Features vs. Competitors:** Sites like Internshala and CollegeDunia have user profiles,
  application tracking, and user-generated reviews.
  - **Improvement:** Prioritize developing a user dashboard and features for submitting company
    reviews and interview experiences.

## SECTION 2 — MANDATORY PAGES FOR ADSENSE APPROVAL & TRUST

You have these pages, which is a great start. Ensure they meet these standards.

- **About Us (`/about`)**
  - **Content:** Your company's mission, story, and photos of the team (even if it's just you).
    Explain _why_ Sproutern exists.
  - **AdSense Check:** Looks for transparency and proof that a real, legitimate entity is behind the
    site.
  - **Mistake to Avoid:** A generic, one-paragraph "About" page. Be detailed.

- **Contact Us (`/contact`)**
  - **Content:** Contact form, professional email (e.g., `https://github.com/itsPremkumar/sproutern-oss`), and a physical or
    business address.
  - **AdSense Check:** Verifies that users can easily reach you. A physical address is a major trust
    signal.
  - **Mistake to Avoid:** Only having a contact form with no other contact details.

- **Privacy Policy (`/privacy-policy`)**
  - **Content:** Must detail what user data you collect (cookies, sign-ups), how you use it, and how
    users can control their data. Mention you plan to use Google AdSense.
  - **AdSense Check:** This is a non-negotiable legal requirement. AdSense will not approve a site
    without a comprehensive policy.
  - **Mistake to Avoid:** Copying a generic template without customizing it to your site's data
    practices.

- **Terms & Conditions (`/terms-and-conditions`)**
  - **Content:** User responsibilities, acceptable use, limitation of liability, and governing law.
  - **AdSense Check:** Shows professionalism and sets legal boundaries for site usage.
  - **Mistake to Avoid:** A vague or incomplete T&C that doesn't protect you or inform the user.

- **Disclaimer**
  - **Content:** A short page or a section in your T&C stating that your content is for
    informational purposes, you are not liable for career decisions, and you may earn from affiliate
    links.
  - **AdSense Check:** Adds another layer of transparency.
  - **Mistake to Avoid:** Hiding it. Make it easy to find from the footer.

- **FAQ Pages (`/faq`)**
  - **Content:** Detailed answers to common questions about using your site, applying for
    internships, and using your tools.
  - **AdSense Check:** Demonstrates user support and high-value, unique content.
  - **Mistake to Avoid:** A short FAQ with only 5-10 basic questions. Make it comprehensive.

## SECTION 5 — INTERACTIVE TOOLS (10 Tools)

1.  **Resume Score Checker:** Analyzes a resume (PDF/text) and gives a score based on keywords,
    length, and formatting.

      session time significantly.
2.  **CGPA to Percentage Converter:** A simple but essential tool for students from various
    universities.
    - **Why it works:** Captures high-volume, low-difficulty keyword traffic.
3.  **Salary Calculator (India):** Calculates in-hand salary from CTC, considering taxes and
    deductions.
    - **Why it works:** High-intent users. Great for SEO and placing high-CPC ads related to
      finance.
4.  **Aptitude Mock Test Generator:** Generates a quick 10-question quiz on quantitative, logical,
    and verbal topics.
    - **Why it works:** Excellent for engagement and session duration. Users will spend 10-15
      minutes per session.
5.  **Interview Question Generator:** Provides tailored questions based on a job role (e.g.,
    "Software Engineer," "Marketing Intern").
    - **Why it works:** Helps users prepare, making your site a go-to resource before interviews.
6.  **Job Description Enhancer:** User pastes a job description, and AI suggests keywords to add to
    their resume.
    - **Why it works:** Positions you as an advanced, AI-powered career platform.
7.  **Cover Letter Generator:** Basic generator that creates a simple cover letter from a few
    inputs.
    - **Why it works:** High utility, drives traffic, and can be a lead magnet.
8.  **Education Loan EMI Calculator:** Helps students calculate monthly payments for education
    loans.
    - **Why it works:** Attracts high-CPC traffic related to financial products.
9.  **Career Roadmap Generator:** Suggests a learning path based on a desired career (e.g., "Become
    a Data Scientist").
    - **Why it works:** Positions you as a career guide, not just a job board.
10. **Typing Speed Test:** A simple test to check words-per-minute.

  - Create 5 detailed "Company Pages" (e.g., TCS, Infosys).

    Ensure you have at least 25-30 high-quality, unique content pieces (blogs, tools, pages). Submit

    the site longer, leading to more ad impressions and a higher Revenue Per Mille (RPM).

  - **Financial Products:** On loan and investment pages, partner with financial aggregators.

- **Email Newsletter Funnels:** Once you have an email list (from Section 6), send a weekly
  newsletter with the best new internships and blog posts. Include a sponsored link or a featured
