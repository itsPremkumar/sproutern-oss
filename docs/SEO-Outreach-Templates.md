# SEO Outreach Templates

Use these email templates to build high-quality backlinks to your content on Sproutern. Replace the
bracketed information `[like this]` with the specific details before sending.

---

## 1. University Career Center Outreach (The `.edu` Link)

**Goal:** Get listed on a University's "Student Resources" or "Career Preparation" page. These are
the highest-quality links you can get (high authority, high relevance).

**Subject:** Resource suggestion for the [University Name] Career Center page

**Body:** Hi [Name of Career Services Director or specific contact],

My name is [Your Name] and I run Sproutern, a free career resource platform for students.

I was reviewing your excellent career resources page here: [Link to their exact page] and noticed
you provide some great tools for students preparing for placements.

We recently published a completely free, updated guide and tool: **"7 Best Free Resume Builders for
Freshers in 2026 (No Login Required)"** which includes an AI-powered ATS optimizer specifically for
tech roles.

Because we focus heavily on helping students pass automated ATS screenings without paying for
expensive tools, I thought it might be a valuable addition to your recommended resources.

You can check it out here: [Link to your blog post or tool]

No pressure to include it, but I wanted to pass it along in case it helps your students prepare for
the upcoming placement season.

Best regards,

[Your Name] Founder, Sproutern [Link to your LinkedIn]

## 2. Broken Link Building

**Goal:** Find a dead or broken link on another blog/website, point it out to the author, and
suggest your working link as the replacement.

**Subject:** Broken link on your post about [Topic of their post]

**Body:** Hi [Author Name],

I was just reading your post on [Title of their post] – really great insights on [Mention 1 specific
point they made].

I wanted to give you a quick heads up that one of the resources you linked to seems to be broken.
The link to "[Anchor text of the broken link]" in the "[Name of section]" section goes to a 404
page.

We actually just published a comprehensive, up-to-date alternative here at Sproutern: [Your Link]

It covers [Briefly mention 1-2 things your post covers] and might make a good replacement for the
broken link so your readers don't hit a dead end.

Either way, keep up the great work with the blog!

Best,

[Your Name] Sproutern

as the source).

**Body:** Hi [Author Name],

article topic].
