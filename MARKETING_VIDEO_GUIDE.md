## ⚡ Step-by-Step Creation Workflow (Using CapCut)

1.  **Gather Assets (15 mins):**
    - Go to **Pexels** and download 3 videos: "Stressed Student", "Happy Student", "Typing on
      Laptop".
    - Go to **Sproutern.dpdns.org** and record your screen (Win+G) scrolling the home page and using the
      Resume tool.

2.  **Open CapCut (New Project):**
    - Import all your videos and screenshots.
    - Drag them into the timeline in the order of the script above.

3.  **Generate Audio (The Magic Step):**
    - In CapCut, click **Text**. Add the text from the "Audio/Voiceover" column.
    - Click **Text-to-Speech** on the right right.
    - Select a voice (recommending **"Jessie"** or **"Chill Girl"** for US accent, or check for
      Indian English accents if preferred).
    - _Pro Tip:_ Once the audio is generated, you can hide the text clips so they don't show on
      screen, or use "Auto Captions" to make them visible subtitles.

4.  **Add Music:**
    - Search "Upbeat Corporate" or "Lo-fi Study" in CapCut's audio tab (or import from YouTube
      Library).
    - Lower the music volume to **-15dB** so the voice is clear.

5.  **Export:**
    - Click Export -> 1080p -> Share to YouTube/Instagram/LinkedIn!
